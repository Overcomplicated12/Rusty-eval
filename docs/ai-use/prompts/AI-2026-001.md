## Exact human-authored prompt

Set up a research-grade generative-AI usage logging system in this repository.

This is compliance/provenance infrastructure only. Do not write any research plan, abstract, paper section, conclusions, future work, or scientific claims.

Policy basis

The researcher is following Society for Science guidance stating, among other things:

AI may be used to generate/develop ideas, with prompt logging where appropriate.
AI-generated initial code is acceptable only with explicit disclosure/citation and a log of prompts.
AI may assist with identifying statistical tests/software tools, with prompts logged.
AI must not write the initial research plan, abstract, paper, poster, conclusions, or future steps.
AI must not generate paper sections that are submitted as the student's own work.
Grammar/syntax-only refinement of student-written material may be acceptable.
AI-generated graphics/images require clear labeling and disclosure.
AI must not be used to generate a starter bibliography or fabricate supporting citations.

Preserve these constraints in project documentation.
