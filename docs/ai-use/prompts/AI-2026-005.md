3. Standard migration buckets

Implement exactly these initial buckets:

TRIVIAL
REFACTOR_THEN_DSL
NEEDS_TRANSPILER
BOUNDARY
UNKNOWN

Keep the bucket definitions documented in one authoritative location.

TRIVIAL

No significant detected migration blocker and appears structurally compatible with the DSL.

REFACTOR_THEN_DSL

Likely migratable after localized mechanical restructuring.

NEEDS_TRANSPILER

Appears conceptually suitable for migration but depends on a currently unsupported or unverified RustyCpp capability.

BOUNDARY

Strong evidence that the declaration belongs to an intentional unsafe, ABI, generated, third-party, or other boundary that should remain outside the safe DSL.

UNKNOWN

Rules cannot justify another classification with sufficient confidence.

UNKNOWN must be preferred over speculative certainty.
