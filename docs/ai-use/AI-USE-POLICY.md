# Generative-AI use policy

This is the project's implementation of Society for Science guidance supplied by the researcher. It is not official legal guidance.

## Allowed with logging and disclosure as applicable

- Brainstorming and idea development.
- Methodological discussion.
- Code generation.
- Debugging assistance.
- Tooling recommendations.
- Statistical-test or software-tool identification.
- Later language refinement where allowed.

Significant human-authored prompts and resulting use must be recorded under this directory. AI-generated code requires explicit disclosure/citation where applicable and enough provenance to locate affected code.

## Not delegated to AI

- Initial research plan, abstract, research-paper text, or poster text.
- Scientific conclusions or future-work conclusions.
- Interpretation of experimental results on behalf of the researcher.
- Starter bibliography generation.
- Fabricated or source-free citations.

The student researcher remains responsible for experimental decisions, verification, data interpretation, conclusions, and final scientific claims. AI references are not verified sources; researchers must examine supporting sources themselves.

## Recordkeeping

Use `AI-YYYY-NNN` IDs. Preserve prompts verbatim only when they were actually captured; never reconstruct or paraphrase an alleged exact prompt. Preserve relevant failed or rejected AI attempts, do not rewrite old records to make results appear cleaner, and never store secrets in the records.
